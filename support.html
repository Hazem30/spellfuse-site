Support

Need help or found a bug?
Send us a message (opens a support request). Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened. Don't include personal information.

How to play

  • Spell: tap 3–8 letters anywhere on the board in order, then press Submit. Each word scores once per run.
  • Merge: select two matching letters of the same tier and press Merge to make one stronger tile (up to tier 4). Stronger tiles make words worth more.
  • Make space: new tiles keep dropping. The run ends when a tile can't fit on a full board. You can pause any time.
  • Rescues: in Endless, a word with 5 different letters earns Row Clear, and every 3 merges earn Freeze. The Daily gives everyone one of each.

Common questions

Is SpellFuse free?
Yes. Every mode, puzzle level and retry stays free. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes between-round ads in every mode and gives six free hints a day instead of three. Rewarded ads stay optional.

When does the Daily board change?
At midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same board, with one official attempt per day and unlimited practice afterwards.

Is my progress saved?
Your progress, records and an unfinished run are saved on your device automatically. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off data sharing?
Open Settings in the app and turn off Share anonymous play data. See the privacy policy.

How do I restore Remove Ads?
Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy it. Restore does not buy it again. If the store is unavailable, your saved access stays in place; check your connection and try again. A refund or revoked purchase takes effect when the app next checks ownership.

What if a purchase is pending or cancelled?
A cancelled purchase gives no paid access. If Apple is waiting for approval, you can keep playing; access applies when the purchase is approved and verified. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.

Do I need an account or a subscription?
No game account or subscription is required. Remove Ads is one purchase through Apple. Restoring that purchase does not restore game progress from another device.

Who verifies my purchase?
Apple handles payment. RevenueCat verifies and restores the lifetime purchase in supported versions; no RevenueCat login is required. Settings → Purchase support is available for eligible Apple buyers, alongside Restore Purchases. It is purchase help, not a subscription. See our privacy policy for purchase processing and optional ad measurement.

How can I ask about billing or refunds?
Use Apple's purchase support for billing or refund requests. Do not post receipts, account details or payment information in public support requests.
